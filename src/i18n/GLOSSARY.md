# Universal PDF — translation glossary

Every translator of this app uses these terms, in the app (`src/i18n/<lang>/`)
and in the store text (`store-assets/strings/<locale>.json`,
`store-assets/play-listing.<locale>.json`, `store-assets/app-store.<locale>.json`),
so the app and its store listing say the same thing.

English (en-GB) is the source. If a term is missing, add it here first, then use it.
UI labels are in sentence case in every language (only the first word and proper
nouns get a capital; German nouns keep theirs).

Notes on the table:
- **Undo vs cancel in French and Italian** are the same word (annuler / annulla).
  Where both appear on one screen, use « Annuler la dernière action » /
  "Annulla ultima azione" for undo only if the icon has no tooltip context.
- **"Sign" means with your hand, not a cryptographic signature.** German uses
  *unterschreiben/Unterschrift*, not *signieren/Signatur*. If a digital
  certificate signature is ever meant, use *digital signieren / digitale Signatur*
  (de), *signature numérique* (fr), *firma digital* (es), *firma digitale* (it),
  *assinatura digital* (pt), *dijital imza* (tr).
- **"Upload" must keep its meaning** ("sent to our servers"). Never translate it
  with a word that could mean import from the device (fr *importer*, it *importa*).
- **"Mark up" in German** is *kommentieren* in running text; the highlighter noun is
  *Textmarker*, a highlight is a *Markierung*.

## Keep in English

Never translate, transliterate or inflect the core of these:

- Universal PDF
- UNI·SIM (keep the middle dot; UNISIM only in the App Store name "Universal PDF by UNISIM")
- Universal ID
- Universal Apps
- Word, OpenDocument, Markdown, GitHub
- AGPL-3.0
- File extensions and formats: .docx, .odt, PNG, JPG, ZIP, PDF, OCR
- The literal confirmation phrase `delete-all`

Articles and case suffixes may attach (fr *l’Universal ID*, de *dem UNI·SIM-Team*,
tr *GitHub’da*, *PDF’i*).

## Per-language style

**French (fr-FR).** Address: *vous*; buttons in the infinitive (« Enregistrer »,
« Annuler »), instructions in the imperative (« Ouvrez un PDF »). Quotes « … » with
a non-breaking space inside; non-breaking space before `: ; ? !`. Typographic
apostrophe ’. Decimal comma (1,5 Mo), date 17/09/2026. "PDF" is invariable
(des PDF). Units: Ko, Mo, Go. Avoid anglicisms where an established term exists
(*pistage*, not *tracking*), but *open source* is standard.

**Spanish (es-ES).** Address: *tú*. Buttons in the infinitive («Guardar»),
instructions in the imperative («Abre un PDF»). Quotes «…» without inner spaces;
opening ¿ ¡ where needed. Decimal comma (1,5 MB), date 17/09/2026. Spain
vocabulary: *móvil*, *archivo*, *rellenar*, *guardar*. "PDF" invariable (los PDF).

**Italian (it-IT).** Address: *tu*. Buttons in the imperative, 2nd person
(«Salva», «Annulla»). Quotes «…». Decimal comma (1,5 MB), date 17/09/2026. "PDF"
and "file" invariable (i PDF, i file). Elide articles before vowels
(l’account, sull’iPad).

**German (de-DE).** Address: *du*, lower case (*du, dein*), as in current app UIs.
Buttons in the infinitive («Speichern», «Abbrechen»), instructions in the
imperative («Öffne ein PDF»). Quotes „…“. Decimal comma (1,5 MB), date 17.09.2026.
Plural PDFs (no apostrophe). Compounds with a brand take a hyphen
(UNI·SIM-Team, PDF-Seiten, QR-Code). Nouns capitalised; otherwise sentence case.

**Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR).** Address: *você* (usually implied by the verb;
drop the pronoun where natural). Buttons in the infinitive («Salvar»),
instructions in the imperative («Abra um PDF»). Quotes “…”. Decimal comma
(1,5 MB), date 17/09/2026. Brazilian vocabulary: *arquivo, salvar, tela, celular,
excluir, baixar, senha, equipe, on-line*. Plural PDFs.

**European Portuguese (pt-PT).** Avoid *tu* and *você*: prefer the infinitive
for labels and headings («Guardar», «Preencher e assinar»), impersonal or
3rd-person polite imperatives for instructions («Abra um PDF»), and passive or
reflexive constructions («a conta pode ser eliminada»). Quotes «…». Decimal
comma (1,5 MB), date 17/09/2026. European vocabulary: *ficheiro, guardar, ecrã,
telemóvel, eliminar, transferir, palavra-passe, equipa, online*. Post-1990
spelling (*diretamente, ação, exceto*). "PDF" invariable (os PDF).

**Turkish (tr-TR).** Neutral polite imperative (*-in/-ın*: «Açın», «Kaydedin»)
for instructions; buttons are the bare verb stem or a noun («Kaydet», «İptal»).
Avoid spelling out *siz/sizin* where the sentence works without it (use
passives: «hesap silinebilir»). Quotes “…”. Apostrophe before suffixes on
abbreviations and proper names, following pronunciation (PDF’i, PDF’e, PDF’ler,
GitHub’da, iPad’de). Decimal comma (1,5 MB), date 17.09.2026. Mind dotted and
dotless i in upper case: İ/i and I/ı (DİĞER, İMZA).

**All languages.** Sentence case for UI labels and headlines. No exclamation
marks. Keep store screenshot headlines to one or two short lines and chips to
one to three words. In store screenshot strings, `*word*` marks the orange
accent: put it on the equivalent punchy word, not a literal position.
